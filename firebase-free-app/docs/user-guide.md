# User Guide

- Sign in with your @spxexpress.com Google account.
- Requester: create an HOD Approval request, upload required PDFs, and submit.
- HOD 1 / HOD 2: open My Approvals and approve, reject, or ask a question.
- Watcher: open My Watches to see requests where you were tagged.
- Everyone: notifications and real-time updates appear automatically.
- Use Review to view a read-only summary.
- Download the PDF once a request is approved.

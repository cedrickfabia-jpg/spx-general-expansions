# Admin Guide

- Open Administration from the toolbar.
- User Access: add users, assign roles, remove or restore access.
- HOD 1 and HOD 2 are unique roles.
- Error Logs: review captured app errors.
- Audit Log: export CSV or PDF.
- Settings: update app name, URL, and archive months.
- Privacy & Compliance: acknowledge the policy.
- Changelog: see recent changes.
